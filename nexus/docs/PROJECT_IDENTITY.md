# 🇳🇵 NepJuris — Product Identity

---

# 🧠 Product Vision

NepJuris is an AI-powered legal intelligence system for Nepal designed to make legal knowledge more searchable, understandable, and accessible through Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and AI-assisted workflows.

The product should feel:

* Professional
* Trustworthy
* Intelligent
* Calm
* Minimal
* Enterprise-grade

NepJuris is not designed as a casual chatbot. It is a serious legal workspace and research assistant.

---

# 🎯 Product Positioning

NepJuris positions itself as:

> A modern AI legal research and intelligence platform tailored for Nepal.

The product experience should communicate:

* reliability
* clarity
* focus
* structure
* confidence

---

# 🎨 Design Philosophy

The design language follows principles inspired by modern enterprise AI products such as:

* Harvey AI
* Linear
* Notion
* Perplexity AI

The interface should prioritize:

* readability
* clean spacing
* low visual noise
* smooth navigation
* focused workflows

---

# 🌗 Visual Identity

NepJuris follows a dark-first design system.

The homepage transitions gradually from dark workspace tones into lighter informational sections to create contrast and storytelling flow.

The transition represents:

* entering the AI workspace
* exploring legal intelligence
* revealing structured knowledge

# 🔤 Typography System

Typography should feel:

* modern
* clean
* authoritative

Rules:

* Large bold headings
* Generous spacing
* Minimal decorative typography
* Avoid overly playful fonts

---

# 📐 Layout Philosophy

NepJuris uses spacious layouts with strong content hierarchy.

The UI should feel:

* open
* readable
* premium
* intentional

---

# 🧱 Component Design Rules

## Cards

All cards must:

* use rounded-xl
* maintain consistent padding
* use subtle borders
* avoid excessive shadows

---

## Buttons

Buttons must:

* maintain consistent height
* use rounded-xl
* follow shared button styles from design system
* avoid inconsistent sizing

---

## Inputs

Inputs must:

* match dark workspace styling
* maintain consistent border system
* use subtle focus states

---

# 🧠 Navigation Philosophy

Navigation should feel lightweight and minimal.

The navbar should:

* remain transparent at top of homepage
* appear/re-render while scrolling upward
* become solid after scroll threshold
* avoid excessive links

Primary navigation should focus on:

* Home
* Docs
* Chat

---

The chat interface should feel like:

* an AI workspace
* a legal research assistant
* a productivity environment

Not:

* a social messaging app
* a casual chatbot

---

# 📚 Docs Experience Philosophy

The Docs page should feel like:

* a searchable legal knowledge base
* an indexed legal archive
* structured information system

It should prioritize:

* discoverability
* clarity
* organization

---

# 🎞 Motion & Animation Rules

Animations should be:

* subtle
* smooth
* purposeful

Avoid:

* excessive motion
* flashy effects
* distracting transitions

Recommended:

* fade-ins
* smooth scroll reveals
* navbar transitions
* hover feedback

---

# 🧠 Engineering Philosophy

Frontend architecture must remain:

* modular
* scalable
* maintainable

UI consistency should be controlled through centralized design tokens and reusable components.

Avoid:

* duplicated styles
* inconsistent spacing
* isolated design decisions

---

* theme.js

The actual implementation source of truth is the design system code.
